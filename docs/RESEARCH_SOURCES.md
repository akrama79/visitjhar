## 1. Primary Official Government Sources

### 1. Department of Tourism, Government of Jharkhand
- **Official URL**: [https://tourism.jharkhand.gov.in/](https://tourism.jharkhand.gov.in/)
- **Authority**: Department of Tourism, Art, Culture, Sports & Youth Affairs, Govt. of Jharkhand.
- **Coverage**: State tourism destinations, geographical coordinates, district profiles, official tourism circuits, approved homestays, and wayside amenities.
- **Verification Impact**: Baseline authority for 40 destinations, 24 district coordinates, and official tourism descriptions.

### 2. Jharkhand Tourism Policy 2021
- **Document URL**: [https://tourism.jharkhand.gov.in/cms/Application/uploadDocuments/download/Document20220726_124458.pdf](https://tourism.jharkhand.gov.in/cms/Application/uploadDocuments/download/Document20220726_124458.pdf)
- **Gazette / Resolution**: Resolution No. 344, dated July 2022.
- **Key Policy Mandates**:
  - Defines 5 core tourism development circuits:
    1. **Religious & Spiritual Circuit**: Baidyanath Dham (Deoghar), Basukinath (Dumka), Parasnath / Shikharji (Giridih), Rajrappa Chhinnamastika (Ramgarh), Itkhori Bhadrakali Temple (Chatra).
    2. **Eco-Tourism & Wildlife Circuit**: Netarhat Queen of Chotanagpur, Betla National Park, Palamau Tiger Reserve, Lodh Falls, Mirchaiya Falls.
    3. **Water Tourism & Dam Circuit**: Patratu Valley & Dam (Ramgarh), Chandil Dam & Dalma (Seraikela/East Singhbhum), Massanjore Dam (Dumka), Tilaiya Dam (Koderma), Getalsud Dam (Ranchi).
    4. **Tribal & Cultural Heritage Circuit**: Maluti Terracotta Temples (Dumka), Amadubi Panikar Village (East Singhbhum), Khunti-Ulihatu Birsa Munda Trail.
    5. **Weekend Leisure & Waterfall Circuit**: Hundru, Jonha, Dassam, Sita, and Panchghagh falls in the Ranchi plateau.
  - Formulates the *Jharkhand Tourist Home Stay Scheme* and community eco-guide certifications.
  - Prescribes strict Adventure & Eco-Tourism Safety Guidelines.

### 3. Official Tourism Downloads, Notices & Circulars
- **Official URL**: [https://tourism.jharkhand.gov.in/downloads](https://tourism.jharkhand.gov.in/downloads)
- **Coverage**: Registered tourism service providers, homestay operational guidelines, district tourist information centres, and environmental preservation notices.

### 4. Directorate of Culture, Government of Jharkhand
- **Official URL**: [https://www.jharkhandculture.com/](https://www.jharkhandculture.com/)
- **Authority**: Directorate of Culture, Govt. of Jharkhand.
- **Coverage**:
  - Documentation of 32 recognized indigenous tribal communities (Santhal, Munda, Oraon, Ho, Kharia, Birhor, Asur, Chero, Bhumij, Mal Paharia, etc.).
  - Intangible Cultural Heritage: Folk dances (Seraikela Chhau, Paika martial dance, Santhali traditional group dance, Karma dance, Jhumar, Domkach).
  - Traditional Visual Arts: Sohrai ritual harvest murals (GI tagged), Khovar bridal chamber murals (GI tagged), Paitkar scroll paintings of Amadubi, Jadopatia scroll paintings of Santhal Pargana.
  - Handicrafts: Dokra lost-wax bell metal casting (Jamshedpur/Dumka/Khunti), bamboo crafts, terracotta relief tiles of Maluti, lac bangles, and tribal silver jewellery.

### 5. Official State Portal of Jharkhand
- **Official URL**: [https://www.jharkhand.gov.in/](https://www.jharkhand.gov.in/)
- **Coverage**: Administrative boundaries of 24 districts, district collectors' offices, state holidays, and gazetted festival schedules.

### 6. Ministry of Tourism, Government of India
- **Official URL**: [https://tourism.gov.in/](https://tourism.gov.in/)
- **Data Portal**: [https://data.tourism.gov.in/](https://data.tourism.gov.in/)
- **Coverage**: PRASHAD scheme projects (Baidyanath Dham spiritual development), Swadesh Darshan tribal circuits, domestic tourist visit statistics, and national eco-tourism guidelines.

### 7. Archaeological Survey of India (ASI) — Ranchi Circle
- **Official URL**: [https://asiranchicircle.in/](https://asiranchicircle.in/)
- **Coverage**: Centrally protected monuments in Jharkhand, including Maluti group of temples (Dumka), Barabar-style rock-cut caves, Benisagar archaeological ruins (West Singhbhum), and ancient temples of Haradih and Diuri.

## 3. Fact Verification Methodology

1. **Coordinates & Locations**: Verified against official Survey of India cartographic data and district portal maps.
2. **Access & Reach**: Road distances verified via National Highway Authority of India (NHAI) and South Eastern Railway network tables.
3. **Safety Warnings**: Water flow hazard warnings at Hundru and Dassam waterfalls documented in accordance with District Disaster Management Authority (DDMA) advisory notices.
4. **Cultural Nomenclature**: Authentic names for festivals (Sarhul, Karma, Tusu Parab, Sohrai) and art forms (Khovar, Paitkar, Dokra) verified against Jharkhand Tribal Research Institute (JTRI) publications.
5. **Attribution & Non-Affiliation**: VisitJhar maintains clear educational, independent research attribution and does not claim official endorsement or government agency affiliation.
